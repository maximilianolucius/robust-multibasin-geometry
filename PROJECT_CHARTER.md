# Project Charter — Robust Multibasin Geometry

## Domain
Applied mathematics at the intersection of fractional differential equations, robust dynamical systems, multistability, basin geometry, persistence/extinction, viability, uncertainty, and strong/Double-Allee dynamics.

## Central research object
Consider a positive fractional family
\[
{}^C D^{\alpha_i}x_i=f_i(x;\theta,m),
\qquad
(\boldsymbol\alpha,\theta,m,\kappa)\in\mathcal U,
\]
where \(\kappa\) may encode an admissible memory-kernel class.

The project studies the robustness of:
- extinction and survival basins;
- bistability;
- basin-relative persistence;
- basin separators;
- common/maximal robust survival and extinction regions.

## Preferred applied realization
A strong/Double-Allee positive system in which extinction and survival/coexistence are genuine competing outcomes.

## Success criterion
Produce a theorem family of strong Q1 applied-mathematics quality that goes materially beyond ordinary robust equilibrium stability or standard robust permanence. Strong targets include:
- persistence of multibasin topology under order/kernel/parameter perturbations;
- sharp or maximal common survival/extinction regions;
- rigorous separator continuation/enclosure;
- exact loss-of-bistability conditions;
- a structured Double-Allee uncertainty specialization.

## Non-objectives
This is not a generic robust-stability paper, an LMI-only paper, a common Lyapunov-certificate paper with no global basin content, or another parameter-sweep study.

## Publication standard
The workflow is governed by agent_directives_publishable_first_submission.md: theorem first, <=25 pages, self-contained, published references only in the submitted paper, no essential supplement, and a strong theorem-driven visual program.
