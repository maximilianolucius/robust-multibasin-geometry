# Inherited Knowledge

## Origin
This project descends from maximilianolucius/fractional-stability-front. The parent project mapped theorem-level fractional stability/dynamical-systems theory, then adversarially tested candidate gaps.

This family survived only after a major narrowing. The broad claim “robust fractional persistence is unexplored” is false.

## Core inherited facts

### 1. Robust linear fractional stability is mature
Published prior already covers:
- uncertain coefficients;
- uncertain fractional order;
- simultaneous order + parameter uncertainty;
- structured perturbations;
- incommensurate systems;
- time-varying interval uncertainty;
- mixed uncertainty.

Therefore uncertain alpha by itself is not novelty.

### 2. Nonlinear robust equilibrium stability is not empty
Published class-specific results provide global robust Mittag-Leffler/asymptotic stability for uncertain nonlinear fractional systems.

Therefore “nonlinear robust fractional stability” is too broad.

### 3. Robust persistence is already abstract
Smith and Zhao (2001) treat robust persistence for semidynamical systems. Salceanu (2022) treats robust uniform persistence for structured delay population models. Classical ecological robust permanence is also highly developed.

A history-state lift does not automatically create a new robust-persistence theorem.

### 4. Fractional viability and memo-viability are direct prior
Caputo viability and fractional differential-inclusion viability have published Nagumo-type/memory-domain theory.

Weak viability — existence of one viable selection — must not be confused with strong invariance or a survival guarantee for every uncertainty realization.

### 5. Strong Allee changes the right global object
Global permanence on all positive states is structurally wrong when a strong Allee effect gives a positive extinction basin.

The relevant object is:
- survival/extinction basin decomposition;
- basin-relative persistence;
- common robust survival/extinction regions;
- robustness of the separator and bistability.

### 6. Order uncertainty is special but not automatically singular
When only alpha varies and the RHS is fixed, equilibrium roots do not move. Stability and memory may change.

Finite-time continuous dependence on alpha is not enough to prove asymptotic basin robustness.

A serious project must compare the lifted dynamics under order/kernel changes in a rigorous common framework.

### 7. The exact search-qualified residual
The parent audit did not identify a published theorem giving, uniformly over simultaneous parameter/order uncertainty:
- common survival regions;
- common extinction regions;
- robust basin-relative persistence;
- sharp basin-separator enclosures;
- preservation/loss of bistability;
- exact/maximal robust threshold sets

for positive uncontrolled Caputo systems with strong-Allee multistability.

This is a search-qualified residual, not proof of absence.

### 8. Conservative certificates are a major risk
A common Lyapunov sublevel set may be useful but may not be mathematically distinctive enough.

The preferred mathematics is structural/sharp:
- maximal robust basin kernels;
- continuity/continuation of separators;
- topology preservation;
- exact conditions for loss of bistability;
- quantified inner/outer enclosures with meaningful sharpness.

### 9. Double/Multiple Allee as structured uncertainty
Multiple mechanisms may provide biologically meaningful correlated uncertainty, but mechanism composition is not independent novelty. It should serve the robust theorem.

## Inherited recommendation
Study robust multibasin threshold geometry under parameter, order and memory-kernel perturbations, with a strong/Double-Allee system as the preferred applied testbed.
