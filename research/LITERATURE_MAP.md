# Literature Map — Robust Multibasin Geometry

## Robust fractional-stability baseline

### Senol et al. (2014)
A Numerical Investigation for Robust Stability of Fractional-Order Uncertain Systems.  
ISA Transactions 53(2), 189–198.  
DOI 10.1016/j.isatra.2013.09.004.

Role: coefficient/order uncertainty baseline.

### Yang & Hou (2019)
Robust Bounds for Fractional-Order Systems with Uncertain Order and Structured Perturbations via Cylindrical Algebraic Decomposition Method.  
Journal of the Franklin Institute 356(7), 4097–4105.  
DOI 10.1016/j.jfranklin.2018.12.024.

Role: coupled order/parameter robust bounds; major novelty killer for uncertain-alpha claims.

### Tavazoei & Asemani (2020)
On Robust Stability of Incommensurate Fractional-Order Systems.  
Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation 90, 105344.  
DOI 10.1016/j.cnsns.2020.105344.

### Zhang & Lu (2023)
Robust Stability of Fractional-Order Systems with Mixed Uncertainties: The 0<alpha<1 Case.  
Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation 126, 107511.  
DOI 10.1016/j.cnsns.2023.107511.

### Song, Wu & Wang (2017)
Lur'e-Postnikov Lyapunov Function Approach to Global Robust Mittag-Leffler Stability of Fractional-Order Neural Networks.  
Advances in Difference Equations 2017, 232.  
DOI 10.1186/s13662-017-1298-8.

Role: nonlinear open-loop robust equilibrium stability is not empty.

## Robust persistence/permanence killers

### Smith & Zhao (2001)
Robust Persistence for Semidynamical Systems.  
Nonlinear Analysis 47(9), 6169–6179.  
DOI 10.1016/S0362-546X(01)00678-2.

### Garay & Hofbauer (2003)
Robust Permanence for Ecological Differential Equations, Minimax, and Discretizations.  
SIAM Journal on Mathematical Analysis 34(5), 1007–1039.  
DOI 10.1137/S0036141001392815.

### Hofbauer & Schreiber (2010)
Robust Permanence for Interacting Structured Populations.  
Journal of Differential Equations 248(8), 1955–1971.  
DOI 10.1016/j.jde.2009.11.010.

### Patel & Schreiber (2018)
Robust Permanence for Ecological Equations with Internal and External Feedbacks.  
Journal of Mathematical Biology 77, 79–105.  
DOI 10.1007/s00285-017-1187-5.

### Salceanu (2022)
Robust Uniform Persistence for Structured Models of Delay Differential Equations.  
Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems - B 27(9), 4923–4939.  
DOI 10.3934/dcdsb.2021258.

Novelty consequence:
history-state robust persistence is already a serious theory.

## Fractional viability / inclusion baseline

### Girejko, Mozyrska & Wyrwas (2011)
A Sufficient Condition of Viability for Fractional Differential Equations with the Caputo Derivative.  
JMAA 381(1), 146–154.  
DOI 10.1016/j.jmaa.2011.04.004.

### Carja et al. (2014)
Viability of Fractional Differential Inclusions.  
Applied Mathematics Letters 38, 48–51.  
DOI 10.1016/j.aml.2014.06.012.

### Girejko, Mozyrska & Wyrwas (2015)
On Memo-Viability of Fractional Equations with the Caputo Derivative.  
Advances in Difference Equations 2015, 58.  
DOI 10.1186/s13662-015-0403-0.

Killer test:
can strong invariance/discriminating-kernel theory plus a Caputo lift already supply the desired robust set?

## Ecological uncertainty and Allee anchors

### Narayanamoorthy et al. (2019)
Analysis for Fractional-Order Predator–Prey Model with Uncertainty.  
IET Systems Biology 13(6), 277–289.  
DOI 10.1049/iet-syb.2019.0055.

This does not resolve robust order/parameter basin geometry.

### Contreras Julio & Aguirre (2018)
Allee Thresholds and Basins of Attraction in a Predation Model with Double Allee Effect.  
DOI 10.1002/mma.4774.

Role: integer-order basin separator/topology baseline.

### Mondal et al. (2025)
Dynamics of a Fractional Order Predator–Prey System with Double Allee Effect and Group Defense.  
DOI 10.1016/j.cjph.2025.09.020.

Role: natural fractional multistable application class.

## Novelty boundary
Not new:
- robust fractional stability;
- uncertain alpha;
- robust persistence in abstract history-state systems;
- viability/inclusion language;
- Double-Allee modeling.

Search-qualified residual:
robust geometry of the extinction/survival basin decomposition itself under parameter/order/kernel perturbations, preferably with sharp/maximal results.
