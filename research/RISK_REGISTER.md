# Risk Register

## R1 — broad theorem already follows from robust persistence
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: compare exact hypotheses with Smith–Zhao, Salceanu and related semiflow permanence theory before proving anything.

## R2 — differential inclusion / discriminating kernel subsumes robust set
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: distinguish weak viability from universal strong invariance; audit adjacent viability theory.

## R3 — alpha perturbation is treated as if it were ordinary vector-field perturbation
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: formalize a common memory-state framework and required topology.

## R4 — only conservative Lyapunov certificates remain
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: target maximal/sharp sets, separator geometry or topology preservation; quantify conservatism.

## R5 — uncertainty grid is mistaken for universal proof
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: interval/analytic universal verification; explicitly label grids numerical.

## R6 — basin classification is horizon-sensitive
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: multiple horizons, trapping neighborhoods, continuation, validated bounds.

## R7 — Double-Allee complexity is decorative
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: use biologically structured uncertainty only if it sharpens or realizes the theorem.

## R8 — kernel uncertainty is too broad or ill-posed
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: introduce kernel uncertainty only after a common state-space/operator class is rigorous.

## R9 — paper becomes control/LMI rather than global applied dynamics
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: keep the novelty centered on extinction/survival multibasin geometry.

## R10 — recent literature closes gap
severity: MEDIUM/HIGH  
mitigation: maintain an adversarial novelty watch; submitted references must still obey the published-only rule.
